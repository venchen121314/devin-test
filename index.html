6 Cafés in Helsinki

A short, opinionated list.

  • Kaffa RoasteryPunavuori

    Working roastery with a counter out front — the place to taste single origins.

  • Johan & NyströmKatajanokka

    Canal-side brick vaults, big windows, and slow brews by the water.

  • Café RegattaTöölö

    Tiny red seaside cottage known for cinnamon buns and an outdoor campfire.

  • Ipi KulmakuppilaKallio

    Relaxed corner café and lunch spot with a community feel.

  • SävyKallio

    Small specialty bar where the baristas take the filter coffee seriously.

  • Good Life CoffeeKallio

    Neighbourhood specialty spot and roaster with a steady local crowd.

Hand-written list — check opening hours before you go.
